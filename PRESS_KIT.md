# Smart Shopper SA - Press Kit

## 📰 **Press Release**

### **FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Smart Shopper SA Launches Revolutionary Grocery Price Comparison App for South Africa**

*New app helps South Africans save money by comparing prices across major retailers*

**Cape Town, South Africa** - Smart Shopper SA today announced the launch of its revolutionary grocery price comparison app, designed specifically for South African consumers. The app allows users to compare prices across major retailers including Checkers, Shoprite, Pick n Pay, Makro, and Woolworths, helping families save money on their grocery bills.

**Key Features:**
- Real-time price comparison across major South African retailers
- Smart shopping list management with voice commands
- Price history tracking and alerts
- Offline functionality for shopping trips
- Loyalty card management
- Dark/Light mode support

**Quote from Founder:**
> "We're excited to launch Smart Shopper SA and help South African families save money on their grocery shopping. With rising food costs, every rand saved counts, and our app makes it easy to find the best deals across all major retailers."

**Availability:**
The app is now available as a Progressive Web App (PWA) at https://smart-shopper-46f4c.web.app and can be installed on any device with a web browser.
